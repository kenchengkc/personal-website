# Research figure sources

- `f1-optimized-cad.png`: Ken Cheng, “A Need For Speed: Enhancing F1 Race Cars with a Novel Computational Fluid Dynamics and Machine Learning Method,” Figure 15 (right), p. 11. The author's optimized SolidWorks rear-wing model. https://drpress.org/ojs/index.php/HSET/article/view/8388
- `f1-cfd-comparison.png`: The same paper, Figure 17, p. 11. Original ANSYS Fluent velocity and pressure comparisons at 33 m/s, with and without the optimized rear wing. Extracted from the published PDF; no synthetic simulation results added.
- `f1-cfd-source-comparison.png`: The author's higher-resolution original Figure 17 composite, copied unchanged from `TSF 3D Simulations (3).png` (1440 × 900). It compares no rear wing with the optimized rear wing at a 33 m/s inlet. Each panel retains its own original legend; the camera angles and color scales differ between cases.
- `f1-optimized-velocity.png` and `f1-optimized-pressure.png`: Unretouched panel extracts from that original composite: pixel bounds (925, 278, 1440, 572) and (925, 573, 1440, 900), respectively. Original contours, color scales, and typography are retained. The animated viewer keeps these source images separate from its illustrative flow tracers. Tracer paths and timing are presentation overlays, not sampled velocity vectors or time-resolved CFD output; the source paper provides static fields. Pressure and Velocity are manually selected views of the optimized model, not animation stages.
- `wasserstein-joint-validation.png`: Exact published aggregate figure at https://wasserstein-regimes.vercel.app/assets/joint-validation.png. Descriptive joint-regime validation and synthetic controls, with no predictive claim.
- `wasserstein-cross-asset-comparison.png`: Exact published figure at https://wasserstein-regimes.vercel.app/assets/cross-asset-comparison.png. Explanation: https://wasserstein-regimes.vercel.app/cross-asset-results/.
- `aster-summary.json`: Saved experiment summary from https://aster-hpo.vercel.app/evidence/smoke/summary.json. Allocation visual follows https://aster-hpo.vercel.app/assets/replication-race.svg and https://aster-hpo.vercel.app/assets/full-budget.svg: 16 candidates, 27 replications each under full budget; the race gives 27 to candidate 00 and 3 to each other candidate. One noisy-quadratic experiment, seed 17. The 100 independent audit evaluations per selected candidate are excluded from the allocation totals.

- `aster-full-budget.svg` and `aster-replication-race.svg`: Unmodified original diagrams from https://aster-hpo.vercel.app/assets/full-budget.svg and https://aster-hpo.vercel.app/assets/replication-race.svg, retrieved October 26, 2026. Original font declarations, labels, geometry, and colors are preserved.
- `gcinf-model-architecture.png`: Author's original `GCINFArch.png`, matching Figure 5, “GC-INF Model Architecture,” p. 2094 (PDF page 5) of “GC-INF: A Novel Adaptive Traffic Control System using Machine Learning for Turning Ratio Predictions,” IEEE ITSC 2023. https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10422188. Copied unchanged from the author's local research materials; original serif labels and colors are preserved.

The research gallery displays the original Wasserstein cross-asset figure, Aster allocation diagrams, and GC-INF model architecture without recoloring, blending, redrawing, or changing their typography. Each figure links to its original full-size asset. The Aster result is one synthetic experiment, not a general speedup claim; independent audit costs are stated separately. Wasserstein's separate forecasting-study conclusion is described in the project record and is not plotted in the cross-asset figure. CML retains its collection-pipeline animation. Need for Speed uses the original CFD fields with a separately labeled illustrative tracer overlay and a full-size source comparison link.
